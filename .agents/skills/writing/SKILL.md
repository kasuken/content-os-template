# Writing ruleset

## Purpose

Write for the actual medium, task, and reader. Do not optimize for sounding human or for evading authorship detection; both lead to worse prose.

## Precedence

When rules conflict: truth, safety, accessibility, and platform or legal requirements come first; then explicit user instructions, genre norms, these rules, and optional heuristics.

## Drafting workflow

1. Identify the medium, audience, reader need, and desired action.
2. For long-form work, choose a through-line and a concrete example or case.
3. Draft to fit that context, using the repository style guide and channel rules.
4. Verify claims, quotes, numbers, causal statements, and links.
5. Revise for specificity, coherence, usefulness, and voice; cut generic or ceremonial language.

## Core rules

- Match formatting to the medium. Use headings and lists where they improve usability; do not add decorative structure to casual prose.
- Prefer concrete, earned specificity over polished generalities. Do not invent facts, quotes, metrics, examples, motives, or authority.
- Use plain language and active verbs. Repeat the ordinary word when it is the right word rather than forcing synonyms.
- Keep closely related thoughts together. Use short sentences for genuine emphasis or a turn in thought, not as a mechanical style.
- Calibrate confidence to evidence. State assumptions and uncertainty plainly.
- Lead task-oriented writing with the answer or next action. For longer pieces, use a deliberate through-line rather than a catalog of topic buckets.
- Avoid canned praise, service-desk closings, slogan-like endings, and repeated three-part cadence.
- Read the finished piece as a first-time reader. Cut filler, unsupported claims, duplicated ideas, and weak transitions.

## Final checks

Before finishing reader-facing prose, confirm:

- It meets the intended reader's need and channel rules.
- Facts and source links are supportable.
- The configured voice and point of view are preserved.
- Accessibility, formatting, and calls to action fit the medium.
- No confidential, personal, or unlicensed material has been included.
